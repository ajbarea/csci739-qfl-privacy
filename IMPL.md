## Now

Trained circuits (#15) on `feat/trained-params`:

- `qflp/data.py`: Bowles et al. benchmark tasks (linear, curves D = 2, 10); `qflp/train.py`: full-batch
  Adam with the learning rate picked on validation accuracy (`results/rq5-training.jsonl`, also the
  logistic baselines).
- `qflp/sweep.py --task --steps --training`: the attack on one training example at several training
  steps; runs differing only in step share the example and the attack starts.
- `qflp/figures.py`: `trained_map`, `trained_table` with an exact McNemar p on paired seeds
  (step 0 vs the last step).

Results (`results/rq5.jsonl`, 720 attacks, 20 seeds, exact gradients, 10 restarts, steps 0/10/300):

- Pooled over twelve settings, 21 seeds recovered only before training and 17 only after, exact
  McNemar p = 0.627. The 1x4 circuits stay recoverable (>= 16 of 20 everywhere); every failure is stuck.
- Per task: linear 5 vs 7, curves D = 10 4 vs 10 (both noise), curves D = 2 12 vs 0 (fewer
  recoveries after training; one task, treat as a lead).
- Prose numbers are macros in `report/tables/numbers.tex`, generated by `qflp.figures`.

`report/report.tex` has RQ1-RQ5 sections; tables and figures are generated.
